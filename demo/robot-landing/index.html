Field notes

What it actually does all day

Follows movement

Two wide-angle optics and a 3-axis neck let it keep eyes on whatever moves. You are seeing the live behavior right now — this page runs the same tracking loop the hardware does.

Learns your hours

It notices when you usually sit down and when you call it a day, and idles quietly in between. All of it stays on the unit — there is no account to delete later.

Minds its business

No microphone, no camera feed leaving the desk, no subscription. The status dial shows exactly what the sensors are doing, in plain light.
